# uwomeds.com

The Hippocratic Council's (Schulich medical student council) website: public council pages, a club directory, room booking and council forms for Schulich medical students on the London and Windsor campuses.

## People and roles

**Student**:
Anyone signed in with an @meds.uwo.ca or @uwo.ca address (or an approved outside email); both domains get the same access.
_Avoid_: User, member

**Club exec**:
A student listed for a club on the current exec roster; may edit only that club's page.
_Avoid_: Club editor, page manager, club admin

**Exec roster**:
The once-a-year spreadsheet of who is an exec for each club (club, name, email, role); being on it is what grants club editing.
_Avoid_: Permissions sheet, club permissions, access list

**Community Relations Director**:
The council position that keeps the exec roster and the club list.
_Avoid_: Community Director

**Page editor**:
A council member allowed to edit the council pages (by default the President, Communications Director and IT Director); signs in like any student, and every save keeps the earlier version.
_Avoid_: Content editor, CMS user

**Booking admin**:
A person who approves or declines room bookings for a campus; may be outside @meds.uwo.ca.
_Avoid_: Booking manager

**IT Director**:
The council position that owns the website and its accounts; `hippoit@uwo.ca` is its permanent role address.
_Avoid_: Admin, webmaster

**Approved outside email**:
An address outside @meds.uwo.ca and @uwo.ca that the IT Director has allowed to sign in.
_Avoid_: Whitelist, exception

## Campuses and bookings

**Campus**:
London or Windsor. A booking may target one campus or both.

**Booking**:
A request to use a room (new, reschedule or cancellation) that a booking admin approves or declines; an approved booking appears on the events calendar.
_Avoid_: Reservation, room request

**Events calendar**:
The public Google Calendar of council and club events, owned by the room booking Gmail account.
_Avoid_: Council calendar, Hippo calendar

**Club event report**:
A club's after-the-fact report of an event's attendance and whether Hippo funded it.
_Avoid_: Event submission, post-event form

## Site operations

**Site email**:
An automatic email the website sends itself (sign-in code, booking update, problem-fixed note). Distinct from class mass emails, which council sends by hand.
_Avoid_: Mailing list, newsletter

**Problem report**:
A report about something broken or wrong on the website, submitted through its Report a problem form.
_Avoid_: Issue, ticket, bug report (from the reporter's side)

**Handover**:
The yearly transfer of the website and its accounts from one IT Director to the next.
_Avoid_: Transition, onboarding

**Beta site**:
The new website running on sample data at a temporary address, shown to council and tested by a few execs before the switch-over.
_Avoid_: Staging, test site, demo

**Switch-over**:
The day uwomeds.com stops showing the old WordPress site and starts showing the new one.
_Avoid_: Cutover, launch, go-live

**Nameserver move**:
Handing uwomeds.com's DNS from Bluehost to Cloudflare with every record unchanged, done on its own weeks before the switch-over.
_Avoid_: DNS migration, domain move

**Domain transfer**:
Moving uwomeds.com's registration (who it's renewed and paid through) from Bluehost to Cloudflare, done by the IT Director in May–early June 2027; separate from the nameserver move.
_Avoid_: Domain move, migration

## MedFriend

**MedFriend**:
The preclerkship study site (lecture notes, question bank, Anki decks) built into uwomeds.com/medfriend behind sign-in.
_Avoid_: Preclerkship site, study portal

**MedFriend reader**:
A student using MedFriend; may open it and download its source.
_Avoid_: MedFriend user

**MedFriend maintainer**:
An exec of the Open-Source Medicine or AI in Medicine club who keeps MedFriend's content current, onboarded through the medwiki contributor flow.
_Avoid_: MedFriend admin, content owner

**Progress carry-over**:
The one-time step on the old MedFriend address's moved notice that brings a student's saved progress, notes and settings from that browser into their account.
_Avoid_: Progress migration, import
